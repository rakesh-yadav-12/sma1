## Title
Analysis of Competitor Activities Using Social Media Data

## Aim
To analyze and evaluate competitor strategies, performance, and engagement patterns using data collected from social media platforms.

## Software Tools
Google Colab, Python programming.

## Theory
Social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn generate large amounts of user interaction data. This data can be analyzed to understand competitor behavior.

### Competitor Analysis
The process of identifying strengths, weaknesses, strategies, and performance of competing brands. It enables businesses to discover market opportunities, mitigate risks, and build a competitive advantage by analyzing customer feedback, pricing, and content strategies.

Competitor analytics helps to identify competitors and research their marketing strategies. The key metrics involved in Competitor analytics include:

- Engagement Rate
- Follower Growth
- Content Reach
- Posting Frequency
- Hashtag Usage

### Types of Analysis

- **Content Analysis**: Type of posts (videos, images, reels, text)
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Positive/negative audience reactions
- **Trend Analysis**: Popular topics and hashtags
- **Benchmarking**: Comparing metrics across competitors
